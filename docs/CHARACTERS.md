
# MONKEY MIND III
## Character Design Specification

### Ainan

Role: Main playable protagonist
Costume: Ivory jacket, crimson scarf, olive cargo trousers
Appearance: Dark wavy hair, beard, sunglasses
Combat: Martial arts, melee attacks and banana weapons
Ultimate: Giant Banana

### Jennifer

Role: Heroine
Costume: Black top and grey jeans
Appearance: Long dark hair and rectangular glasses
Purpose: Story cinematics and rescue sequence

### Maliha

Role: Main antagonist and final boss
Costume: Traditional clothing with dupatta
Appearance: Fuller body shape, dark hair, no glasses
Combat: Melee, brain projectiles, electricity and curses
Behaviour: Dynamic movement, dodging and counterattacks

### Mini Maliha

Variants:
- Brain Thrower
- Yapper
- Shocker
- Brawler

### Asset Requirements

- Original character artwork
- Transparent PNG sprite sheets
- Consistent 128 x 128 animation frames
- Consistent foot anchoring
- Separate portraits and gameplay sprites
- Consistent pixel density
- No decorative backgrounds inside sprite sheets
- No static portraits used as playable fighters

### Combat Animation Standards

- Anticipation
- Active attack frames
- Impact
- Recovery
- Directional movement
- Damage reaction
- Knockout animation
